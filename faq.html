Questions

The things people ask in the first few weeks. Anything else, email moorefamilyfitness18@gmail.com.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing resets. Skip it and do today’s session; there’s no make-up workout. If you miss three or more training days in a row, the next session is lighter on purpose. After a week or more away, your first week back repeats the previous phase’s workload.

Is the first week supposed to feel this easy?

Yes. The first two weeks rebuild the routine and get your body used to training again, so every set stops well short of failure. It gets harder each phase. Adding extra work now is how a lot of comebacks stall in week three.

What counts as a full week?

Every training session that week, plus at least 90% of your daily habits. Up to two short versions count as full sessions; a third counts as half. A week that falls short isn’t lost, it just isn’t counted as full.

When should I use the short version?

On crowded days, not tired ones. It’s the first four movements with one set fewer, about 20–25 minutes. Tap “Short on time?” above the workout.

How do I know when to add weight?

Log the weight and reps for every set. When every set reaches the top of the rep range, the line under the movement tells you to go up by the smallest step next time. Inside the range, stay at that weight and add a rep. Fall short twice and it suggests about 10% lighter.

A movement doesn’t feel right, or I don’t have the equipment.

Tap Swap on that movement and pick another one for the same slot, for this phase or always. Settings also has your equipment and lower back, knee and shoulder adjustments. Stop and swap any movement that causes sharp or spreading pain, numbness, tingling or weakness in an arm or leg. If you’ve been inactive for a long time or were told to limit activity, check with your doctor before starting.

I’m sick, injured or travelling. What do I do?

Set a status at the bottom of Today: Away, Sick or Injured. Reminders pause and those days show as a break, not as missed. Tap “I’m back” when you’re ready, and the program picks up on today’s date.

Do I have to start on a Monday?

No. Day 1 can be any day, and you choose your rest day. Day 1 is always Strength A, and the other sessions fill the week around your rest day. Change either in Settings → Program.

Can I choose my own habits?

No. The five habits are part of the challenge and the same for everyone. The one setting is your daily steps, from 5,000 to 10,000, in Settings → Program. Tap “What counts?” above the checklist to see what each habit means.

Where is my data? What if I get a new phone?

Everything you log stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it too. Before you switch phones, use Settings → Backup → Export backup, then Restore on the new phone.

What happens after week 2?

The first two weeks are free with every feature. After that, one payment of $24.99 unlocks weeks 3–10, yours to keep. If you stop at week 2, your place waits for you, and nothing you’ve logged is ever locked.

What happens after Day 70?

You’ll be able to run the 10 weeks again for free, starting from the weights you finished on. Onward Pro, coming later, adds new programs to move on to.
